# Careers and YC Signals

## Rain hiring signals

Rain's careers pages indicate active work in:

- backend AI systems and customer-facing agentic payments;
- internal AI operations and agent/skill libraries;
- card authorization, ledgers, controls, and lifecycle;
- multi-chain smart contracts and transaction processing;
- real-time fraud and anomaly detection;
- embedded compliance, licensing, and audit readiness.

Relevant skills repeatedly include Node.js, APIs, data pipelines, tool calling, structured outputs, evaluation frameworks, permissions, Solidity, EVM tooling, real-time processing, SQL/Python, and compliance controls.

This favors a practical prototype with deterministic checks, working integrations, evaluation, and an auditable transaction path.

## Monad hiring/ecosystem signals

Monad's agentic-payment documentation and hackathon bounty point toward:

- x402 and MPP machine payments;
- low-cost, high-frequency settlement;
- agent developer tooling;
- EVM smart contracts and familiar Ethereum tools;
- agent identity, reputation, and auditable onchain activity.

The Mac Mini bounty rewards the best use of Monad with Rain. Monad therefore needs a visible and necessary role in the demo.

## YC request-for-startup signals

Relevant YC themes include:

- renewed opportunities to build in crypto as infrastructure matures;
- AI-native compliance infrastructure;
- software built for autonomous agents rather than merely human users;
- new financial and commercial infrastructure required when machines transact.

These themes support the broad category but do not validate this specific product or buyer.

## Existing startup signal: Sponge

Sponge demonstrates that founders and investors already believe agents need wallets, cards, payment protocols, spending controls, and merchant gateways.

The implication is not to rebuild Sponge. The opportunity is to move one level higher into purchase correctness, delivered-outcome verification, audit evidence, and reconciliation.

## Skills required to build the startup

An initial team would need:

- one strong backend/fintech engineer for payments, ledgers, webhooks, and integrations;
- one agent/AI engineer for tool calling, structured mandates, evaluations, and workflow reliability;
- one product-oriented founder able to sell to finance, procurement, and agent-platform teams;
- access to compliance, fraud, security, and payments expertise as the product moves beyond a demo.

For the hackathon, two or three experienced engineers can divide the work into agent/policy, Rain integration, and Monad/demo UI.
